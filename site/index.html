Local-first mail and calendar

Your mail, on your computer. Instantly.

Familiar is a desktop mail and calendar client that keeps everything in a local store, so every screen opens in milliseconds and works offline. It has a complete command line, so you and the AI tools you choose can use it too.

Get Familiar on GitHub How your data is handled

Why Familiar

One Rust engine underneath a sync daemon, a command line, an MCP server and a desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Blazingly fast

Every screen and every command reads a local SQLite store, never the network. Sync runs in the background, so nothing you do waits on a server.

100% command-line coverage

Everything the app can do, fam can do. Every action is one typed command, and the CLI, its JSON schema, the MCP tools and the app are all generated from it.

Runs on any OS

Windows, macOS and Linux, built and tested on all three. The daemon and CLI have no GUI dependencies, so they also run headless on a server or in a small container.

Restorable from one file

Accounts, signatures, calendars, views, rules, shortcuts and agent permissions live in one config.toml. Copy it to a new machine, sign in, and your mail syncs back.

Built for you and your AI tools

Because every action is a command, Familiar works as well for an AI agent as for a person. You decide what an agent may do.

  • Over 50 commands across mail, drafts, folders, calendar, contacts and sync, each with --json output and stable exit codes.
  • MCP server built in. fam mcp serve offers every command as a tool to Claude and other MCP clients.
  • Safe by default. Agents can read and save drafts. Sending, deleting and other writes need a grant you add to your config, and every write is recorded in an audit log.
  • Optional AI features, such as finding calendar events in an email or a second opinion on spam, are off until you turn them on.
$ fam mail search "from:school newsletter" --json
$ fam draft reply 4182 --body "Thanks, see you then"
$ fam cal list --days 7

# Let Claude Code use it, read and draft only
$ claude mcp add familiar -- fam mcp serve --allow read,draft

Your accounts

Familiar signs in to your mail provider with its official sign-in page and talks to it directly from your computer.

Microsoft

Outlook.com, Hotmail and Microsoft 365: mail, calendars and contacts, through Microsoft Graph.

Gmail and Google Calendar

Gmail support is being added through Google's Gmail, Calendar and People APIs: reading, labelling, archiving and sending your mail, managing your calendar events, and suggesting your contacts as you type. As with every account, your Google data goes only between your computer and Google.

Your mail never touches our servers

There are no Familiar servers to touch. Mail, calendars and contacts are synced straight from your provider to a store on your own computer. Sign-in tokens stay in your operating system's keychain. Familiar has no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Read the privacy policy for the details, including how Google user data is handled.
